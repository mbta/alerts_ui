## Summary

The bug was that special characters (e.g. bullet •, em-dash —, curly quotes) in
alert `header_text` or `description_text` appeared garbled when downloading the
alert history CSV export. The root cause was that the original export used plain
UTF-8 without a BOM, so Excel applied Windows-1252 encoding and turned the
multi-byte UTF-8 sequences into junk characters.

The fix was to switch the controller to use `NimbleCSV.Spreadsheet` which emits
UTF-16 LE with a BOM (`FF FE`). Spreadsheet apps detect the BOM and decode
correctly, preserving all Unicode characters.

**Conditional pass** because: on dev-green no existing alert has bullets or
em-dashes in its text fields, so the specific character round-trip could not be
directly verified against the deployed environment. All 81 dev-green CSV
downloads are correctly encoded as UTF-16 LE with BOM and no garbling was
detected. The round-trip with actual special characters was verified via ExUnit
unit tests against local.
